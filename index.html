How the 100 score is calculated

Each hour starts at 100. Points are removed for choppier waves, swell, stronger wind, gusts, rain risk, cold sea temperature, showers and lower tide. Higher tide is preferred, so low-water slots can lose up to 10 points.

Wave above 0.35 m: stronger penalty Swell above 0.25 m: moderate penalty Wind above 13 km/h and gusts above 22 km/h: gradual penalty Rain risk above 35%: gradual penalty Sea below 10 deg C: cold-water penalty Lower tide: up to 10 points off Showery weather: 8 points off
